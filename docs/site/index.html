A small tool.
A shorter trip back.

  1. Choose your display

    Click the monitor preview or use the dropdown. Identify displays puts labels on your physical monitors.

  2. Gather your windows

    Bring movable windows from the other displays into your current workspace. Recovery is saved before movement.

  3. Restore what you borrowed

    Return gathered windows to their saved placements. Destination-resident windows remain untouched.

Unplugging isn't
forgetting.

If the gathering destination disconnects, Window Gather makes one automatic attempt to return available borrowed windows.

Unavailable or failed returns stay pending. Reconnect the monitor, then explicitly Restore. Reconnection alone never moves your windows.

Closed applications cannot be reopened by recovery. Elevated or protected applications may refuse movement. Pending errors remain visible.

Keep it close, not in the way.

Closing the window hides it in the notification area. Reopen it from the tray, or use Exit to stop the app.

Your shortcuts, your choice.

Gather defaults to Ctrl + Alt + F11. Change shortcuts if registration warns you; Windows reserves the preserved F12 Restore default for debuggers.
